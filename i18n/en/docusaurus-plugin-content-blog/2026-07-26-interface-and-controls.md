The Lumina Studio Wiki now has a complete interface and control reference. You can look up individual switches, parameters, and workspaces to learn when they appear, what they change, and how they interact with other settings.

<!-- truncate -->

## What is new

- **69 Chinese reference pages** and their **69 English counterparts**, covering the workspace, image conversion, colour matching, previews, model features, vectorisation, materials, LUT tools, and system settings;
- [public contribution guidance](https://github.com/lumina-layer-studio/Lumina-Studio-Wiki/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for community authors and developers, with shared rules for bilingual pages, images, links, and local checks.

## Where to begin

If you need to understand a specific button or parameter, open the [interface and control reference](pathname:///docs/reference/) and browse by feature group. If you need to complete a full task, start with Tutorials and Workflows instead.

Software releases remain in the Changelog. Wiki Updates covers new tutorials and important documentation revisions, keeping product changes separate from editorial updates.
