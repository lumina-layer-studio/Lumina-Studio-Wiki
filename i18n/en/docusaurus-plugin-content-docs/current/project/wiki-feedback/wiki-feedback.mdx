# Wiki Feedback and Suggestions

This page collects feedback about the Lumina Studio Wiki. Tell us when wording is difficult to understand, a workflow needs more images, or the topic you need is missing.

Please leave a comment at the bottom of this page. You do not need to understand Markdown, code repositories, or the Wiki file structure, and you do not need to prepare a complete rewrite.

## What can I report?

- wording that is too technical or uses unexplained terminology;
- missing control locations, steps, comparisons, or real print examples;
- instructions that no longer match the current version;
- inaccurate explanations or conclusions;
- broken links, images, or videos;
- information that is difficult to find through navigation or search;
- unclear Chinese or English wording;
- a missing feature, workflow, or troubleshooting topic;
- any other issue that makes the Wiki harder to use.

## What makes feedback easier to act on?

Include as much of the following context as you can:

1. the affected page title or public URL;
2. the paragraph, image, or step that caused the problem;
3. what was confusing or inaccurate;
4. whether you want a clearer explanation, screenshot, comparison, example, or different organization.

You do not need to know the correct answer. Explaining what was difficult and what you expected to see is already useful.

## Copyable comment template

```text
Affected page:
Feedback type: unclear / missing image or comparison / inaccurate or outdated / broken link or media / navigation or search / translation / missing topic / other
Location on the page:
Problem or experience:
Suggested direction:
```

## Example

> Affected page: Hue Protection<br />
> Feedback type: missing image or comparison<br />
> Location on the page: “How matching changes”<br />
> Problem or experience: the text explains the matching logic, but I cannot tell what will visibly change in an image.<br />
> Suggested direction: add side-by-side previews of the same image with Hue Protection disabled and enabled, and mark the areas with the clearest change.

## Report software failures through Lumina Studio

This page is only for Wiki content, organization, and reading experience. If Lumina Studio reports an error, fails to generate or download a result, or behaves incorrectly, follow [In-app Bug Reports and Reproducible Information](../../reference/workspace-and-navigation/bug-report/bug-report.mdx) and submit the report through the application.

## Check privacy before posting

Comments are public. Do not post accounts, email addresses, passwords, access tokens, private links, local paths, server addresses, device identifiers, or other personal or confidential information. Crop or hide unrelated details when an example is necessary.

## What happens to feedback?

Maintainers will organize the feedback, verify the current software behavior, and include suitable suggestions in later Wiki revisions. Similar reports may be handled together. A comment does not guarantee adoption, a response, or a fixed publication date.
