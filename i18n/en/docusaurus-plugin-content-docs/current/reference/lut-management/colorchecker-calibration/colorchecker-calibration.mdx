# ColorChecker Calibration

With ColorChecker Calibration enabled, an uploaded calibration-board photograph first enters the calibration dialog. The
confirmed full-resolution result is then passed to LUT Extractor; calibration is not performed on the UI thumbnail alone.

## Reference and placement

Choose a built-in or custom reference. A reference contains patch Lab values, white point, and a content hash. An uploaded
`.xlsx` creates a session custom reference. Auto detection and calibration stay disabled until the reference is ready.

Auto Locate returns four corners and confidence. Corners can also be placed and dragged manually. Patch order supports
normal, 180° rotation, horizontal flip, and vertical flip. Verify the rectified preview against the selected reference.

## Sampling and fitting

- **Sampling method:** mean or median.
- **Sample radius:** centre area sampled from each patch.
- **Output blur:** optional smoothing of the corrected output.
- **Gray normalisation:** automatic, enabled, or fallback.
- **Calibration algorithm:** 3×3 matrix, 3×4 matrix, root polynomial, or automatic selection.

A larger sample is not automatically better: touching patch edges, glare, or shadows contaminates the fit.

## Confirming the result

The result reports mean, median, P95, and maximum ΔE00, plus the worst patch and gray checks. Excluded patches or risky
neutral-reference deltas appear as warnings. Accept passes the image to extraction; Retry returns to the calibration
parameters without applying the unconfirmed result.

ColorChecker calibration can reduce camera and illumination bias. It cannot repair clipping, severe glare, missed focus,
or an incorrectly printed board.
