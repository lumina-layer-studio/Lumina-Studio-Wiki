# Generate a LUT Calibration Board

A calibration board arranges the layering recipes of a selected color mode into printable color blocks. After printing and photographing the board, you can sample the measured colors in LUT Extractor to build a custom LUT.

<img

  alt="LUT calibration-board parameters"
/>

## Parameters

- **Color Mode:** Selects the recipe set and page count. Supports BW, 4-color, 5-color Extended, 6-color, and 8-color modes.
- **Block Size:** `3–10 mm` in `0.5 mm` steps (default `5.00 mm`). Larger blocks make camera focusing and center sampling easier while increasing overall board dimensions; reduce the size when build-plate space is tight.
- **Block Gap:** `0.4–2.0 mm` in `0.01 mm` steps (default `0.84 mm`). Separates adjacent blocks and preserves the grid boundary, helping prevent color bleed or edge-shadow overlap.
- **Backing Thickness:** `0.4–3.5 mm` in `0.08 mm` steps (default `1.20 mm`). Increasing thickness improves board rigidity and reduces light bleed from behind.

Selecting an 8-color mode exposes the **Full Export** toggle. Because 5-color Extended and 8-color calibrations contain more recipes than fit on a single plate, they split across multiple pages and export as a multi-page package.

## Output

After generation, the right panel displays the full-board preview alongside per-layer thumbnails; click any thumbnail to inspect that layer at full size. Direct slicer actions (such as **Generate & open in Bambu Studio**) use the printer, nozzle, and slicer configured in **Settings**.

Keep the outer positioning border intact when photographing and cropping the board. During extraction, select the exact color mode and page number used to generate each board, and keep each extraction session limited to photos from the same calibration mode.
