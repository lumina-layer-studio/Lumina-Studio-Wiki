# LUT Compare

LUT Compare joins two LUTs by identical recipe and calculates ΔE00 between their predicted colours. It is designed for
material-batch changes, repeat-calibration consistency, and before/after comparisons.

## Statistics

- **Matched recipes:** records that can be joined by recipe key.
- **Identical RGB:** matched records with exactly equal RGB values.
- **Mean / P95 / maximum ΔE00:** central, tail, and worst shared-recipe differences.
- **Coverage A / B:** shared recipes as a percentage of each LUT's comparable records.

P95 means 95% of shared recipes are at or below that difference and is usually more representative than the single
maximum. The maximum remains useful for finding the most abnormal recipe.

## Compatibility warnings

Warnings report mismatched colour mode, layer count, layer height, line width, schema, predictive capability, or dynamic
capability. With no shared recipes, row numbers must not be treated as equivalent recipes.

Largest Differences displays both colours, the recipe, and ΔE00. Comparing LUTs does not modify, merge, or overwrite
either source.
