# 2D Preview and Hover Inspection

The 2D preview displays the exact color result used for 3D modeling and lets you pick colors, select connected regions, and inspect optical filament layers directly on the canvas.

## Zooming and comparing with the original

- **Zoom:** Scroll with the mouse wheel or trackpad to zoom between `50%` and `1000%`.
- **Pan and reset:** Right-click and drag to pan across the canvas; double-click to reset the zoom level.
- **Compare original:** Hold the compare button in the upper-right corner—or hold `Shift` when focus is outside a text field—to temporarily view the uploaded source image. Releasing the button or `Shift` (or switching window focus) immediately restores the processed preview.

Pixel-crisp rendering keeps individual preview pixels sharp at high zoom so you can inspect exact region edges without blur.

## Hover inspector

Hovering over any pixel on the canvas displays:

- Exact pixel coordinates;
- Predicted surface color;
- Filament color on every optical layer at that point.

Use the **Magnifier** and **Layer Colors** toggles in the middle column to show or hide either section independently. Layer images load on demand when first inspected, and transparent pixels or empty layers appear blank. **Layer Colors** reflects the optical stack order in the Lumina recipe; final physical AMS slot assignments are mapped in your slicer.

## Cyan selection outlines

Clicking the canvas outlines your target based on the active [selection mode](../selection-modes/selection-modes.mdx). Region-based tools trace the connected component around the clicked pixel in cyan, whereas **Same color** highlights all matching pixels across the entire image.

When you select an individual area from **Gradient Groups**, Color Workstation outlines only that region's exact spatial mask rather than every pixel sharing the same HEX value elsewhere in the artwork.
