# Quick recolor & similar recipes

Open **Color Adjustment** after generating a preview to find **Quick recolor & similar recipes** in the right column. Select the region you want to modify on the canvas, then pick a target color or apply a nearby filament stack. Edits apply only to the active selection while leaving matching colors elsewhere untouched.

## Select the area to edit

| Tool | Selection behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Same color | Selects every region matching the clicked color across the image |
| Similar colors | Selects visible regions within the active ΔE00 tolerance |
| Pick blocks | Adds or removes individual connected regions one click at a time |
| Selection brush | Selects every connected block touched by your brush stroke |

You can browse target colors and candidate recipes at any time, then select an area on the canvas to apply them.

## Similar-color tolerance

**Tolerance** measures perceived color difference in ΔE00. Raising the value expands the selection to include a wider range of nearby shades:

- Defaults to **6**, adjustable from **0** to **30** in steps of **0.5**.
- At **0**, only regions with the exact clicked color are selected.
- Higher values group neighboring tonal steps together, and may also include nearby background or adjacent details if their colors are close.
- Clicking the same color again toggles the selection for that range.

Adjusting tolerance updates only the selection boundary; verify the highlighted outline on the canvas before applying a replacement.

## Set target color B

Enter a six-digit HEX value such as `#ff8800`, open the color picker, or click one of the swatches under **Pick from colors already in the image**. With a LUT source, Lumina locates the closest printable LUT color to **B**. With a Material Archive source, **B** serves as the target color while the actual filament stack is solved from your active materials.

Review the nearest printable match, click **Replace selection with this color**, and check the updated preview alongside surrounding unselected areas.

## Sample a color from the image

Click the eyedropper button (**Pick from image**), then click anywhere on the 2D preview. The displayed print color at that pixel is copied into **Target color B** without altering your current selection. Press `Esc` or collapse the card to exit eyedropper mode.

## Choose a similar recipe

Under **Similar recipes**, switch between **From selection** and **From color B**—if your selection spans multiple colors, click one swatch to set the reference color. Badges next to each entry indicate how the color was derived:

- **Measured:** An existing measured entry in the active LUT.
- **Predicted:** Estimated by the forward prediction model.
- **In image:** Already used elsewhere in the current preview.

Layer chips run from left to right in **viewing face → bottom** order. Inspect the ΔE value and filament sequence, then click **Apply**. In Material Archive mode, this list draws from colors and stacks already present in your current preview.

## Review and save

Wait for the 2D and 3D previews to refresh after applying a change, or click **Undo** to step back. If applying a recipe updates region boundaries, re-select the target area before making further edits. Before clicking **Generate**, verify the modified regions and layer stacks, and [save the project](../../../knowledge/share-card-json/share-card-json.mdx).

An empty recipe list indicates no matching entries are available for the active color source; if loading fails, run **Preview** again as prompted. Always recheck suggested recipes after changing filaments or switching color sources.
