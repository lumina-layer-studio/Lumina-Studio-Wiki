# LUT Color Grid

After previewing with a Legacy LUT, the right column of Color Workstation loads every color that LUT can produce, grouped into **Used in image** and **Other available**. Selecting a source color on the left adds **Recommended Replacements (12)** at the top, ranked by RGB distance.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search bar and filter pills to narrow down the palette:

- **HEX:** Full or partial hex codes, such as `#8B5055` or `8b50`.
- **RGB:** Comma-separated values, such as `139, 80, 85`.
- **Hue filters:** Quick filters for **Red**, **Orange**, **Yellow**, **Green**, **Cyan**, **Blue**, **Purple**, and **Neutral** shades, plus **Favorites**.

**Used in image** reflects the active preview palette, while **Other available** lists all remaining entries in the LUT. Recommended replacements are ranked by numerical color distance; physical appearance also depends on viewing light, build-plate surface, and filament batch.

## Favorites and Card view

Double-click any swatch to add or remove it from **Favorites**. Favorites are saved locally in your browser per LUT so separate libraries stay independent.

LUTs with physical layout metadata let you switch between **Swatch** and **Card** views. **Card** view mirrors the physical calibration pages, rows, columns, and A/B labels recorded in the LUT for side-by-side comparison with printed swatches. LUTs without layout metadata stay in **Swatch** view.

## Confirming a replacement

Clicking a target swatch stages a `source → target` preview at the top of the palette column:

- Click **Confirm Replace** to recalculate the 2D and 3D previews.
- Click **Cancel** to discard the staged color without changing the session.
- Selections created from a spatial region keep their exact pixel mask rather than remapping matching HEX values across the entire image.
- Multi-color and multi-region selections are applied together in a single batch.

Material Archive mode uses the dynamic solver in [Achievable Material Colors](../material-achievable-colors/material-achievable-colors.mdx) instead of a fixed LUT grid.
