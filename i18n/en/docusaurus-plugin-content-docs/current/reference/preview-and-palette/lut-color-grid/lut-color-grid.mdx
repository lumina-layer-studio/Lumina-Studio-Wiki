# LUT Colour Grid

After previewing with a traditional LUT, the right column loads every colour that LUT can produce. Colours used in the
current image are separated from the remaining entries. Selecting a source colour adds twelve recommendations ordered by
RGB distance.

## Finding a colour

The search field accepts:

- HEX, such as `#8B5055` or `8b50`;
- RGB, such as `139, 80, 85`;
- hue filters for red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, and neutral colours.

Used in Image comes from the active preview palette. Other Available contains the remaining entries from the same LUT.
Recommendations are numerical neighbours, not a guarantee of the closest physical print under a particular light, build
plate, or filament batch.

## Favourites and display modes

Double-click a swatch to favourite or unfavourite it. Favourites are stored locally and separately for each LUT.

Compatible LUTs can switch between Swatch and Card views. Card view follows the physical page, row, column, and A/B labels
recorded in the LUT. A LUT without card-layout metadata remains in Swatch view.

## Confirming a replacement

Clicking a target colour creates a pending source-to-target item:

- Confirm Replace recalculates the 2D and 3D previews.
- Cancel leaves the session unchanged.
- A gradient-region source keeps its exact spatial mask instead of falling back to a whole-image HEX remap.
- Multiple colours and regions are handled in one batch.

Material archives have no fixed LUT grid and use
[Achievable Material Colours](../material-achievable-colors/material-achievable-colors.mdx) instead.
