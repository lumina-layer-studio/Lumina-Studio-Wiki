# Achievable Material Colors

After previewing in **Material Archive** mode, the right column of Color Workstation calculates achievable colors dynamically from your active filament setup. Every query uses the selected materials and their order, color-layer count, layer height, and substrate—run **Preview** and query again whenever you change any of these settings.

## Entering a target color

Pick a color with the system color picker or type a three- or six-digit HEX code such as `#8CE` or `#88CCEE`, then press `Enter` or click **Suggest colors**. Three-digit codes expand automatically to six uppercase digits, and invalid HEX strings are ignored.

The solver returns up to **20** candidates ordered by ΔE00 distance from your target. Because it enforces a minimum color separation between swatches to prevent near-duplicate results, queries may return fewer than 20 swatches.

Each swatch displays its predicted color and ΔE value; hover over a swatch to inspect its full HEX code, ΔE, and layer recipe indices. ΔE compares the optical model's prediction against your target color, while physical prints also vary with filament batch, layer height, build-plate texture, and viewing light.

## Query sources

The status line above the swatches indicates how the results were computed:

- **Cache hit:** Loaded from an exhaustive cache matching the active materials, layer count, layer height, substrate, and grid density.
- **Runtime hit:** Queried from a saved 32-million-candidate smart recipe cache.
- **MITM live solve:** Searched up to 2 million combinations on the fly when no matching cache was available.

Live solving keeps everyday queries fast and responsive. When high material or layer counts push total combinations past the 2-million live ceiling, build an exhaustive cache or the 32M smart recipe cache first to expand coverage and speed up lookups. Caching changes only search speed and coverage without altering your material profiles.

## Applying a suggested color

If you already have a valid source color or region selected on the left, clicking **Suggest colors** stages the closest candidate automatically; click any other swatch to stage that color instead. Click **Confirm replacement** to rebuild the 2D and 3D previews, or **Cancel** to clear the staged choice.

Replacement scope follows your active selection mode in Color Workstation:

- **All / Same color:** Replaces every pixel using the selected source colors across the image.
- **Current / Region / Pick blocks:** Updates a single connected region.
- **Multi:** Applies the replacement to all selected regions in one batch.
