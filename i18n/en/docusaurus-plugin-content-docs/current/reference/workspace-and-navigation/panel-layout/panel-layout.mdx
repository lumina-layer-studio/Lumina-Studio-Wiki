# Show, Collapse, Move, and Reset Panels

In Converter, **Core Settings** and **Creative Center** are organized into movable panels. Panels can dock along the left or right edge of the window or float freely over the canvas when you want an unobstructed 3D view.

## Panel Controls

Click **Panel Controls** in the top bar to view the panels used by the **active primary page and active secondary page**:

- A blue dot indicates that a panel is currently visible.
- Click a panel name to hide or show it.
- Switching secondary pages updates the list to match that view.
- **Color Adjustment** uses a full-screen workspace without dockable panels.

Hiding a panel only removes it from view—its feature stays active and all parameter values remain intact when you show it again.

## Move and dock

Drag any panel by its title bar. Releasing it near the left or right window edge snaps it into that dock at the drop position, and panels in the same dock stack cleanly without manual alignment.

When you resize the window, Lumina recalculates panel positions automatically. Any floating panel that would fall outside the visible viewport returns to the left dock so controls stay reachable.

## Collapse and expand

Click the arrow on the right side of a panel's title bar to collapse or expand it. You can also:

- Double-click the title bar.
- Focus the title bar with the keyboard and press `Enter`.

Collapsing hides the panel body while keeping its title bar in place. While dragging, Lumina temporarily measures the panel at its collapsed height for smooth placement, then restores its previous expanded state and height once dropped.

## Layout memory

Panel coordinates, dock side, collapsed state, and visibility are saved in local browser storage. Because they are workspace preferences rather than project parameters, they are excluded from share cards, JSON files, and 3MF exports, and do not sync across devices.

After an app update, stored entries for retired panels are cleaned up automatically, and newly introduced panels appear in their default positions.

## Reset Layout

**Reset Layout** restores every panel to its default position, visibility, and collapsed state while keeping your active project intact:

- Uploaded artwork and generated previews stay in place.
- LUT, material-archive, and structure settings remain unchanged.
- Color replacements and custom layer recipes are preserved.
- Printer, nozzle, and slicer selections in Settings are untouched.

Use **Reset Layout** anytime panels overlap, drift out of place, or end up in an inconvenient dock order.
