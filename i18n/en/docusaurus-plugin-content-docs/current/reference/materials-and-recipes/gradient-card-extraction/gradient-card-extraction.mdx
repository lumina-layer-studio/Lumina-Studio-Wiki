# Gradient Card Extraction

Gradient Card Extraction fits a reusable layer-prediction archive from a white-substrate and a black-substrate card
printed with the same material.

<img
  src={require('./assets/01-gradient-card-inputs.webp').default}
  alt="Kit ZIP, material name, and image inputs"
/>

## Required files

Provide the Kit ZIP from [Gradient Card Generation](../gradient-card-generation/gradient-card-generation.mdx), a complete
photo of the white card, and a complete photo of the black card.

Use the same camera, exposure, white balance, and calibration settings for both photos. Avoid glare, clipped highlights,
and shadows over sample patches. If the current environment cannot decode HEIC/HEIF, convert the files to PNG or JPG.

## Image source and ColorChecker

The default source is a direct camera or scanner capture. Enabling ColorChecker Calibration lets you correct and confirm
the white and black images separately; accepted images are explicitly recorded as corrected sources.

Repeated verification runs can merge only when their source class matches. Raw captures and ColorChecker-corrected
images are not mixed into one history set.

## Rotation, corners, and anchor patches

After upload, rotate either image by 90° when needed. Mark the card corners in this order:

1. top-left;
2. top-right;
3. bottom-right;
4. bottom-left.

<img
  src={require('./assets/02-corner-controls.webp').default}
  alt="Pure-patch position and corner nudge controls"
/>

Pure White Block Position and Pure Black Block Position determine the sampling order. Keep Auto Detect unless
orientation detection is unreliable, then identify the corner containing the exposed pure substrate patch.

Once corners exist, select corner 1–4, choose a `1–10` pixel nudge step, and move it with the directional buttons. Keep
the quadrilateral on the usable card area rather than including the table, fingers, or outside background.

## Extract and save

<img
  src={require('./assets/03-extract-action.webp').default}
  alt="Black card area and Extract Material action"
/>

Extract Material becomes available after the ZIP, both images, and both four-corner sets are complete. Results include
the perspective-corrected sampling boxes, estimated RGB/HEX, mean and maximum ΔE, linear and per-channel RMSE,
white/black orientation confidence, and raw fit data.

A material name is required before saving. Brand is optional and may reuse an existing brand or create a new one. When
re-verifying an existing archive, choose Merge or Override as explained in
[Editing and Re-Verification](../material-edit-and-verification/material-edit-and-verification.mdx).

During re-verification, **Cancel Re-Verification** exits the active verification target without deleting the existing
library record. **Merge Result** adds the new samples to history of the same source class; **Override Result** replaces
the archive's current fitted data. The details view can also download `stage_A_parameters.json` to preserve the fit
parameters and diagnostic summary from this run.

Review the saved archive in the [Material Library](../material-library/material-library.mdx), then validate its stack
prediction with [Material Recipe Query](../material-recipe-query/material-recipe-query.mdx).
