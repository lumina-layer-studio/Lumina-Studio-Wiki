# Fridge Magnet cavities

Fridge Magnet mode cuts one or more magnet cavities into the backing. Enabling it switches Structure Mode to Single-sided and turns off Puzzle, Large Format, and Keychain Loop.

## Presets and custom sizes

Round presets include D6×2, D8×3, D10×3, and D12×3 mm. Rectangular presets include 10×5×1, 15×5×2, and 20×10×2 mm. Changing shape loads that shape's default preset; editing any dimension switches the item to Custom.

Custom ranges:

- Round diameter: 1–50 mm
- Rectangular width and height: 1–80 mm
- Thickness: 0.5–3.1 mm
- Center X and Y: -999–999 mm

These values set the cavity dimensions in the mesh. Measure your physical magnets and print a quick test piece to verify fit and assembly clearance before running a full batch.

## Multiple magnets and layout



**Add Magnet** inserts a default round magnet and arranges all items automatically. **Auto Layout** keeps each magnet's dimensions and recalculates center positions. **Remove Selected** deletes only the selected item in the list. Selection is shared between the list and the 3D scene; clearing selection leaves the magnet in place.

## Automatic backing thickness

Lumina reserves roughly **0.4 mm** of shell beneath the thickest magnet and rounds to **0.08 mm** layer increments. When needed, the backing thickens automatically up to the 3.5 mm limit, and the panel summary displays the resulting thickness.

Before printing, inspect the cavity roof, bottom shell, and perimeter edges layer by layer in your slicer to confirm that no cavity breaks through the display surface.
