# Interface scale

In the desktop app, **Interface scale** adjusts the display size of text, buttons, and panels without affecting the physical dimensions of your model or exported 3MF.

Open **Settings → General** and choose 80%, 90%, 100%, 110%, 125%, 150%, or 175% under **Interface scale**. At 100%, Lumina follows your operating system's display scaling. Pick a higher percentage for larger controls or a lower percentage to fit more workspace on screen. Your preference is saved automatically across app restarts.

/>

You can also step zoom up or down with `Ctrl` plus `+` or `-` on Windows and Linux, and press `Ctrl + 0` to reset to 100%. On macOS, use `Command` instead of `Ctrl`.

This setting applies to the desktop app. When using Lumina in a web browser, use your browser's built-in page zoom; browser zoom and desktop scale are stored independently.
