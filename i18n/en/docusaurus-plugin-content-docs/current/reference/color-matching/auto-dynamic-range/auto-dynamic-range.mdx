# Auto Dynamic Range

**Auto Dynamic Range (Experimental)** compresses source image lightness into the brightness range reproducible by the active LUT or Material Archive before color quantization and recipe matching. Use it when an image spans brighter highlights or deeper shadows than your loaded filaments can physically produce.

This option is off by default.

## Location

Open **Converter → Core Settings → Advanced Settings**, then locate **Auto Dynamic Range (Experimental)**.

## How it works

When enabled, Lumina Studio processes image lightness in five steps:

1. Reads the minimum and maximum CIELAB L\* values from the active LUT or material palette.
2. Measures the actual L\* range present in the source image.
3. Compresses lightness only when the target range is narrower than the source range.
4. Applies smooth toe and shoulder curves to shadows and highlights, preserving the midtone slope under mild compression.
5. Preserves the a\* and b\* chroma channels, then proceeds to color quantization and recipe matching.

Rather than applying a flat linear fade toward gray, mild compression rolls in the extremes while keeping midtones intact. When a much narrower target range requires stronger compression, midtone contrast scales down proportionally so shadow and highlight gradation remains distinguishable.

## When compression is skipped

Lumina Studio leaves image lightness unchanged when any of the following applies:

- The active color source does not provide a valid reachable lightness range.
- The reachable lightness range is equal to or wider than the source image range.
- The source image spans less than 1 L\* (essentially flat lightness).
- The target range leaves no effective room for compression.

In any of these cases, toggling the switch produces the same preview.

## Evaluating the result

Compare near-white highlights and near-black shadows before and after enabling the option. Compression helps active materials separate subtle tonal steps at both ends of the range, though overall contrast may appear slightly softer. This setting does not alter the LUT color count or material layer heights.

Rebuild both the preview and the 3MF model after toggling this switch. Lumina recipe cards do not save this experimental setting.
