# Stroke, Thin Lines, and Coverage Fix

**Output Enhancement** eliminates hairline gaps between adjacent color regions and helps preserve fine linework from the source image.

  src={require('./assets/01-output-enhancement.webp').default}
  alt="Image Vectorizer output enhancement controls"
/>

## Enable Stroke and Stroke Width

When **Enable Stroke** is on, each SVG path is exported with a matching stroke alongside its fill. **Stroke Width** ranges from `0–20` and defaults to `0.5`:

- **Moderate values** overlap neighboring paths slightly, hiding hairline white seams during rendering or scaling.
- **Excessive values** expand strokes into neighboring regions and thicken fine details.

Turning **Enable Stroke** off hides **Stroke Width** and exports fill-only SVG paths.

## Thin Line Max Radius

Ranges from `0.5–10 px`, with a default of `2.5 px`. Using a distance transform, the vectorizer identifies narrow strokes up to this radius and protects them from breaking apart during segmentation and fitting:

- **Too low**: delicate linework may drop out or fragment.
- **Too high**: ordinary narrow regions may also be treated as thin-line features.

This radius is measured in pixels on the internal working image, not in millimeters on the printed model.

## Enable Coverage Fix and Min Coverage Ratio

When **Enable Coverage Fix** is enabled, the engine measures total canvas coverage after contour tracing. Whenever coverage falls below **Min Coverage Ratio**, it patches uncovered pixels to seal tiny gaps between regions.

**Min Coverage Ratio** ranges from `0–1`, with a default of `0.998`. Values closer to `1` trigger gap patching more readily.

Coverage repair is designed specifically to close tiny inter-path gaps. If the shape boundaries themselves follow the wrong contours, adjust [Superpixel Segmentation and Antialiasing](../vectorizer-segmentation/vectorizer-segmentation.mdx) or [Curve Fitting and Region Filtering](../vectorizer-curve-and-filtering/vectorizer-curve-and-filtering.mdx) first.
