# Which Features Can Be Used Together?

In Lumina Studio, **color edits and surface add-ons can generally be combined freely**, while **features that alter 3D geometry or export packaging follow clear mutual-exclusion rules**:

- **Composable color and surface options**: **Color Workstation**, **Custom Recipe**, **Outline**, **Transparent Coating**, and **Separate Backing** work together on standard single- and double-sided projects.
- **Structural and workflow modes**: **Relief**, **Fridge Magnet**, **Puzzle**, **Large Format**, and **Batch** modify underlying geometry or export pipelines and follow specific compatibility rules.
- **Fridge Magnet exclusions**: **Fridge Magnet** requires a single-sided structure and cannot be combined with **Puzzle**, **Large Format**, or **Keychain Loop**.
- **Puzzle exclusions**: **Puzzle** cannot be combined with **Batch**, **Large Format**, **Fridge Magnet**, or **Keychain Loop**.
- **Relief and Cloisonné**: **Relief** is unavailable in **Vector** mode, and the **Cloisonné** entry is hidden in the current release.
- **Slicer verification**: Whenever you combine structural add-ons, export a fresh 3MF and inspect the sliced toolpaths layer by layer.

## Grouping Features by What They Change

### 1. Color and Layered Recipes

Global replacements, local block edits, and **Custom Recipes** in **Color Workstation** adjust how color layers use your selected filaments. They pair cleanly with **Outline**, **Transparent Coating**, **Separate Backing**, and standard structural features. Simply click **Generate** after any color edit to export an updated 3MF. See [Color Workstation Basics](pathname:///en/docs/knowledge/color-workstation-basics/).

### 2. Surface Add-Ons and Object Organization

These options add or split meshes in the exported 3MF while preserving the base model structure:

- **Outline**: Traces a border along clean transparent edges (images without an alpha boundary or SVGs with tangled geometry may produce incomplete outlines).
- **Transparent Coating**: Adds a dedicated top-surface mesh—map this object to clear filament in your slicer.
- **Separate Backing**: Exports the backing plate as its own object for independent filament or infill control without changing **Backing Thickness**.
- **Free Color**: Extracts a matched color's regions into a standalone object so you can assign a dedicated spool in your slicer.

You can stack several of these options together; expand the full object tree in your slicer afterward to confirm every mesh's filament assignment.

### 3. Structural Modes and Specialized Exports

**Relief**, **Keychain Loop**, **Fridge Magnet**, **Puzzle**, **Large Format**, and **Batch** reshape 3D geometry or change how files are packaged. Lumina enforces mutual-exclusion rules among conflicting options—and if you import an older recipe file containing conflicting toggles, Lumina automatically disables one to keep the project state valid.

## Five Common Starting Setups

### Flat Display Plaques and Prints

Pick **High-Fidelity**, **Pixel Art**, or **Vector** mode, verify your base **Preview**, and refine colors in **Color Workstation**. Once the color match looks right, add **Outline**, **Transparent Coating**, or **Separate Backing** one step at a time.

### Keychains and Pendants

Use a single-sided structure with **Keychain Loop**, then add **Outline** or **Transparent Coating** if desired. In your slicer's Layer Preview, zoom in on the loop joint to confirm a solid, continuous bridge to the main body and an unobstructed hole (leave **Fridge Magnet** and **Puzzle** off).

### Fridge Magnets

Select a single-sided structure and enable **Fridge Magnet**. You can pair it with **Color Workstation**, **Outline**, and **Transparent Coating** (while keeping **Keychain Loop**, **Puzzle**, and **Large Format** off). Make sure **Backing Thickness** leaves plenty of solid wall behind the magnet recess without cutting into the fine color layers.

### Relief Models

Start with a bitmap image and a single-sided structure, enable **Relief** first to verify height direction, and then optionally add **Outline** or **Transparent Coating**. **Vector** mode does not support Relief, and **Batch** mode supports color-driven relief heights rather than separately uploaded height maps.

### Puzzle, Large Format, and Batch Workflows

Run each of these as its own dedicated export workflow:

- **Puzzle**: Keep **Large Format**, **Batch**, **Fridge Magnet**, and **Keychain Loop** turned off, and inspect interlocking tolerances after slicing.
- **Large Format**: Validate colors and layer settings on a normal single piece first, then follow [Large Format: Tile Size, Assembly Order, and ZIP Output](pathname:///en/docs/knowledge/large-format/) to configure tile bounds and slice each tile.
- **Batch**: Test your shared color source and parameters on one representative image in single-image mode before loading the full queue.

## Why Toggling One Feature Turns Another Off

To prevent invalid 3D meshes, Lumina automatically disables conflicting structural options when you toggle a mutually exclusive mode:

- Enabling **Fridge Magnet** switches to single-sided mode and turns off **Puzzle**, **Large Format**, and **Keychain Loop**;
- Enabling **Puzzle** turns off **Large Format**, **Keychain Loop**, and **Fridge Magnet**;
- Enabling **Relief** switches to single-sided mode (and turned off **Cloisonné** in earlier versions);
- Entering **Batch** mode disables **Puzzle**.

Check the settings summary after switching structural modes to confirm which options are active.

## Backing Layer-Height Modifier Boundaries

The **Backing Layer Height Modifier** is built for standard flat backings opened in compatible Bambu-family slicers. Keep these exceptions in mind:

- **Gradient Calibration Cards** use fixed layer calibration heights and do not use the backing modifier;
- **Puzzle** and specialized exports manage their own structural slicing parameters;
- **Double-sided, Relief, and Fridge Magnet** models should be checked layer by layer in slicer Preview to confirm that coarse backing layers never encroach on color layers or magnet cavities;
- **Separate Backing** splits the backing into its own mesh, whereas **Backing Layer Height Modifier** adjusts Z-range layer thickness—enable either or both as needed.

See [Backing, Color Layers, and Layer-Height Modifiers](pathname:///en/docs/knowledge/base-color-layer-height/) for details.

## Checklist When Combining Features

1. **Verify the base Preview**: Check your modeling mode, color source, dimensions, and single/double-sided structure first.
2. **Add features one at a time**: Inspect 2D, 3D, and **Layer Preview** after each addition.
3. **Export a fresh 3MF**: Click **Generate** after every parameter or color change.
4. **Open as a full project**: Expand the slicer's object tree to check the backing, color meshes, Free Color objects, coating, outline, and attachments.
5. **Map physical spools**: Match every project filament to the real spool and hardware slot on your printer.
6. **Inspect sliced toolpaths**: Rotate to the display face and scrub through every layer to check filament swaps, local layer heights, and attachment joints.
7. **Print a quick test piece**: For magnet fit, narrow keychain loops, double-sided coatings, or new filament sets, run a small test sample before committing to a large print.

For full slicer import and slot-mapping steps, see [Import a Lumina 3MF into Your Slicer Correctly](pathname:///en/docs/tutorials/slicer-material-mapping/).

## Common Questions

### Can I combine Outline, Transparent Coating, and Keychain Loop?

Yes—those three work well together on standard single-sided pendants. In your slicer, verify that the outline preserves fine silhouette details, the coating object is assigned to clear filament, and the loop neck prints with continuous perimeters.

### Can I put both a magnet recess and a keychain loop on the same piece?

Lumina treats **Fridge Magnet** and **Keychain Loop** as mutually exclusive. If you want a dual-purpose piece, export the model with **Keychain Loop** enabled and add a cylindrical negative part on the back face directly in your slicer to cut the magnet pocket.

### Why is Relief missing when Vector mode is selected?

**Relief** builds continuous height variations from raster pixel data, whereas **Vector** mode extrudes flat path regions. Upload a PNG, JPG, or WebP bitmap in single-sided mode to use **Relief**.

### Why does my Large Format SVG look rasterized along tile edges?

**Large Format** splits clean vector paths natively whenever possible, falling back to high-resolution raster tiling when an SVG contains unsupported features or extreme path complexity. Inspect edges in Preview and test-print one tile first if sharpness is critical.

### Why did an imported recipe turn off one of its saved switches?

If a shared recipe comes from an older version or contains options that conflict under current rules, Lumina disables the conflicting toggle so the project can generate cleanly. Review the active settings summary and **Preview** after importing.

### If my slicer finishes without warnings, is the feature combination ready to print?

Clean slicing confirms valid 3D mesh geometry, while practical details—magnet floor thickness, keychain loop strength, clear coating assignment, and display-face orientation—still require a quick layer-by-layer check in slicer Preview.
