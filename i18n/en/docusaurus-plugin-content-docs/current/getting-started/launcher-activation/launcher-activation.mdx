# Activate Lumina Studio with the Launcher

:::note[Open-source code and the launcher]
Lumina Studio is developed openly in the [Lumina-Layers repository](https://github.com/lumina-layer-studio/Lumina-Layers) and is free for both personal and commercial use. Running from source does not require launcher activation. This guide covers verifying existing purchase or membership access in Lumina Launcher.
:::

When you open Lumina Launcher on a new computer, verify your purchase or Patreon membership once to download, install, and launch Lumina Studio directly from the launcher.

## Before you begin

1. Download and open Lumina Launcher from an official Lumina channel.
2. Make sure your computer is connected to the internet.
3. Prepare the credential matching the platform that processed your payment:

| Payment channel | What you need | Where to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afdian | Afdian order number | Paste into **Order number or activation code** |
| Card payment on Lumina website (Waffo) | Order reference starting with `LUM-` | Paste into **Order number or activation code** |
| Patreon | Patreon account with active membership | Click **Activate with Patreon** |

If you started on the Lumina website and completed checkout on Afdian or Patreon, follow the steps for that platform. Standalone Lumina activation codes also go into **Order number or activation code**.

## Activate with Afdian

### 1. Find your Afdian order number

1. Sign in to the Afdian app or [Afdian website](https://ifdian.net/) with the account used for your purchase.
2. On desktop web, open **Dashboard** → **My Orders**. In the mobile app, open your profile and tap **Orders**.
3. Under **Paid**, locate your Lumina Studio order and click **Copy** next to **Order No.** (or open the order details to copy the full number).

<img src={require('./assets/afdian-entry.webp').default} alt="Afdian Dashboard in the desktop navigation" />

<img src={require('./assets/afdian-orders.webp').default} alt="My Orders and Paid on Afdian" />

<img src={require('./assets/afdian-copy.webp').default} alt="The Afdian order number and Copy control, with the order details concealed" />

Copy the **Afdian order number**, not the WeChat Pay or Alipay receipt transaction ID. If you checked the order on your phone, you can sign in to Afdian in your desktop browser to copy it directly on your computer.

### 2. Activate your device

1. Paste the order number into **Order number or activation code** in Lumina Launcher (characters appear as dots for privacy).
2. Click **Activate this device**. Once verified, the launcher opens its home screen.

## Activate with a Lumina website card order

### 1. Copy your `LUM-` order reference

1. Open the Lumina payment result page in the browser where you completed checkout.
2. Copy the full **Order reference** starting with `LUM-` (include the `LUM-` prefix; do not use your bank or card processor's transaction ID).
3. Make sure the status reads **Payment confirmed**. If it still shows **Payment submitted**, keep the reference and retry once confirmation completes.

If you closed the result tab, check your browser history in the same browser. If you still cannot locate the reference, contact official support with your proof of payment.

### 2. Activate your device

1. Paste the full `LUM-` reference into **Order number or activation code** in Lumina Launcher.
2. Click **Activate this device** to complete verification.

## Activate with Patreon

Patreon verification uses browser OAuth sign-in rather than an order number.

### 1. Confirm which Patreon account holds your membership

If you have more than one Patreon account:

1. Sign in to [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/), open **Settings** → **Memberships**, and locate **Lumina Studio**.
2. Click **View Details** to confirm this account holds your eligible paid tier.

<img src={require('./assets/patreon-memberships.webp').default} alt="The Memberships entry in Patreon settings" />

### 2. Authorize in the launcher

1. Click **Activate with Patreon** on the launcher activation screen.
2. Sign in to your Patreon account in the browser window that opens and approve access.
3. Return to Lumina Launcher. Once verified, the launcher opens its home screen. If authorization is canceled or times out, click **Activate with Patreon** again.

<img src={require('./assets/launcher-patreon.webp').default} alt="The Activate with Patreon button in Lumina Launcher" />

## Install and launch Lumina Studio

Once your device shows **Activated**, install the application:

1. On **Home**, select an available release and click **Install**.
2. After installation completes, click **Launch Lumina**. Installed builds can also be managed from **Versions**.

## Troubleshooting

### The launcher stays on "Verifying order"

Keep the launcher online and wait a moment for verification to finish. If it remains stuck, take a screenshot and contact official support.

### Activation failed

- **Afdian**: Confirm you copied the Afdian order number (not a payment-app transaction number) from the account that purchased Lumina Studio.
- **Website card order**: Confirm the full `LUM-` prefix is included and payment is confirmed.
- **Patreon**: Confirm your browser signed in to the Patreon account holding an eligible Lumina Studio membership.

### Activated, but no versions appear

Click **Check for updates** to refresh the release list. If the launcher reports that activation was saved but the version list could not refresh, simply retry later—you do not need to activate again.

### "Activate with Patreon" is missing

Download and install the latest Lumina Launcher from an official channel.

### Contacting support

Include your payment channel, launcher version, and a screenshot of any error message. Send order numbers or payment receipts only through private official support channels, never in public comments or group chats.
