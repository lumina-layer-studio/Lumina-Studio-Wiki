# First Use: Configure Your Slicer, Printer, and Nozzle

When you open Lumina Studio for the first time, set your slicer, printer model, and nozzle diameter to match your hardware. Lumina Studio uses these three settings to size build plates, compute pixel line scales, and generate compatible 3MF projects.

> Screenshots below show **BambuStudio**, **Bambu Lab P1S**, and a **0.4 mm** nozzle as examples—select the software and hardware you actually run.

## Before you start

Have three details ready:

- Your everyday slicer
- Your exact printer model
- The nozzle diameter currently installed on your printer (check your printer screen or the marking stamped on the nozzle if unsure)

## Setup steps

### 1. Open System Settings

Click **Settings** in the top navigation bar.

<img
  src={require('./assets/settings-device-en-01-open-settings.webp').default}
  alt="Open Settings at the top of the page"
/>

### 2. Open the Slicer menu

Locate **Slicer** under System Settings and open the dropdown menu to see supported slicers.

<img
  src={require('./assets/settings-device-en-02-open-slicer.webp').default}
  alt="Open the slicer menu"
/>

### 3. Select your slicer

Choose the slicer you use for printing.

<img
  src={require('./assets/settings-device-en-03-select-slicer.webp').default}
  alt="Select the slicer you actually use"
/>

### 4. Select your printer model

Click your exact printer model in the device list.

<img
  src={require('./assets/settings-device-en-04-select-printer.webp').default}
  alt="Select your actual printer model"
/>

### 5. Select the installed nozzle size

On the selected printer card, click the nozzle diameter installed on your machine.

<img
  src={require('./assets/settings-device-en-05-select-nozzle.webp').default}
  alt="Select the installed nozzle size"
/>

### 6. Review the summary

Check the summary bar at the bottom to confirm your slicer, printer model, and nozzle size. Changes are saved automatically—there is no separate Save button.

<img
  src={require('./assets/settings-device-en-06-check-summary.webp').default}
  alt="Check the equipment settings summary"
/>

Whenever you switch slicers, printers, or nozzles later on, update **Settings** first before generating new 3MF files.

## Troubleshooting

### My printer is not listed

Available printer profiles depend on the selected slicer—double-check Step 3 first. If your printer still does not appear, a built-in profile for that model is not yet included in your current build.

### Why is there no Save button?

Selections take effect and persist immediately. Once the summary matches your setup, you can head straight back to the workspace.
