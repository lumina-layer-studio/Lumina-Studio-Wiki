# Lumina Studio 2.0 Development Report: March 2026

> Reporting period: March 9–31, 2026

[← Changelog overview](/en/docs/releases/overview) · [Next: April 2026 →](/en/docs/releases/2026-04)

## Monthly overview

Following the 1.6.3 release, Lumina Studio moved into 2.0 development. Work in March replaced legacy export, SVG, and preview pipelines that limited speed and maintainability, while expanding image-format support, material presets, and slicer profiles to establish the architectural baseline for 2.0.

## Highlights

### Faster SVG preview and generation

Multiple SVG path-handling defects were fixed and key preview stages were rebuilt, cutting a representative workflow from roughly 92 seconds to about 10 seconds. Complex SVG artwork reaches color matching and 3D preview much sooner while preserving full final print accuracy.

<!-- devlog-entry:2026-03-svg-preview-performance prs:1,11 -->

### Native 3MF export pipeline

The legacy Python-assembled XML/ZIP export path was replaced with a native library, reducing overhead on large models and laying the foundation for watermarks, slicer metadata, and multi-material project structures.

<!-- devlog-entry:2026-03-native-3mf-export prs:4,13 -->

## New features

- Added DNG raw image upload support so camera RAW captures can enter calibration and color-extraction workflows directly.
  <!-- devlog-entry:2026-03-dng-upload prs:10 -->
- Added XYD Xiaoming PLA LUT presets for 4-, 6-, and 8-color workflows.
  <!-- devlog-entry:2026-03-xyd-lut-presets prs:9 -->

## Slicers and 3MF

- Added Anycubic Slicer Next and Kobra printer profiles with tailored 3MF project metadata.
  <!-- devlog-entry:2026-03-anycubic-kobra-support prs:14 -->
- Added 3MF watermark support for identifying exported files and tracing their origin.
  <!-- devlog-entry:2026-03-3mf-watermark prs:13 -->

## Calibration and materials

- Unified channel ordering across 6-color calibration boards and recipes, preventing color shifts between extraction, layer previews, and exported models.
  <!-- devlog-entry:2026-03-six-color-consistency prs:16 -->

## Stability, performance, and infrastructure

Additional engineering work covered pipeline debug previews, automatic temporary-file cleanup, frontend API routing, launcher process cleanup, and regression assertions—reducing leftover files and processes ahead of April's workflow migration.

<!-- devlog-entry:2026-03-engineering-foundation prs:3,5,6,8,12,15 -->

## Known limitations

- 2.0 remained in early architectural migration this month; vector, material-profile, and modeling workflows were still being unified.
- New slicer profiles awaited broader verification across hardware and nozzle combinations.
