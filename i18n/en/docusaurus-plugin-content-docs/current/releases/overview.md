# Lumina Studio Changelog

This page tracks Lumina Studio public releases and monthly 2.0 development updates.

:::info[Development status]
Lumina Studio is developed in the open at the [Lumina-Layers repository](https://github.com/lumina-layer-studio/Lumina-Layers). The monthly reports below summarize completed features and fixes from each development cycle.
:::

## Latest progress

### [October 2026: Open source, conversion presets, and print projects](./2026-10.md)

Updated through October 30. Lumina Studio moved to open-source development and published the 2.0.1 source release, adding conversion presets and desktop interface scaling, streamlining color source, crop/rotate, and export controls, and fixing print-project and Color Workstation issues.

Reports covering late July, August, and September are also complete. For the latest commits and releases, visit the [Lumina-Layers repository](https://github.com/lumina-layer-studio/Lumina-Layers).

## Public 1.x releases

Starting in January 2026, the 1.x series introduced the unified application, live 3D previews, multicolor LUTs, six- and eight-color calibration, vector modeling, free colors, 2.5D relief, and direct slicer integration. Subsequent 2.0 development is tracked in the monthly reports above.
